# For Clara — 17 ♡

A layered birthday experience made for Clara, starting with her 17th birthday wish and slowly building toward the question.

## Layers

1. Birthday opening
2. How we met in SKY
3. Little memories
4. Birthday letter
5. Personal video
6. The question
7. Ending based on her answer

## Personal assets

Add the supplied portrait as `assets/clara.jpg` and the personal video as `assets/your-video.mp4`.

The site works without either asset, showing an elegant fallback instead.

## Interaction

- Tap/click the buttons to progress.
- Scroll, swipe, or use Arrow/Page keys to move between layers.
- The proposal has separate **Yes** and **I need a little time** endings.
- Sound is optional and starts only after the visitor taps the sound control.

Designed as a cinematic, dark, romantic birthday/proposal experience rather than a conventional single-page scroll site.
